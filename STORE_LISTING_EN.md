# GIFMaker.SHU - Screen and photos straight to GIF

## Short description
Stack screen recordings and photos on one timeline, add captions and stickers, and save it all as a single GIF.

## Detailed description
GIFMaker.SHU is a lightweight Chrome extension that turns your screen and your photos straight into a GIF with no intermediate MP4 file. Everything is processed inside your browser.

- Launch from the toolbar icon or Ctrl+Shift+G on any page
- Choose a tab, window, or full screen to record
- Drag to crop an exact area, or keep the full screen
- A 3-second countdown before recording starts, so you can switch to the tab you want and press play — capture keeps running while you are on another tab
- Record up to 60 seconds at 5–12fps
- Stack as many photos and recordings as you like on one timeline and combine them into a single GIF
- Reorder or delete scenes, set how long each photo shows, and trim recorded clips
- Add captions and stickers per scene
- Output at 640–1280px or original size, with 64–256 colors
- No sign-in, no account, no uploads
